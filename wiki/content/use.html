Use

Open Options, then Resource Packs, and click Download VT Resource Pack.

Do thisTo
Click a categoryShow its packs and subcategories.
Click Installed, at the bottom of the sidebarShow packs in your Vanilla Tweaks zips
Cmd/Ctrl+1 to 9Open the first to ninth category
Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+TabOpen the next or previous sidebar row
Click a packSelect it, or deselect it
Shift-click a packSelect or deselect a range
Cmd/Ctrl+ASelect every pack in the list. Again to deselect.
Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+ADeselect every pack in every category
Click the arrow on a packOpen its page
Arrow keysMove the outline over the packs. Right opens a pack's page.
Space or EnterSelect or deselect the outlined pack. Hold Shift for a range.
Cmd/Ctrl+FSearch every category
EscClose a pack's page, or the search
Back and Forward, Cmd/Ctrl+[ and ], or the mouse's side buttonsMove through the categories you've visited
List and grid buttonsSwitch how packs are shown
Drag a panel's edgeResize it
Drag a panel's edge to the window's edgeHide the panel
Click the arrow on the window's edgeBring a hidden panel back

Search

Every word must appear in a pack's name or description.

Panels

Drag a panel's edge to resize it. Under a 400-pixel window width, Selected packs drops out.

Conflicts

Packs that conflict turn the same colour once you've picked both.

Installed

Installed shows the packs in every Vanilla Tweaks zip in your resource pack folder. With more than one zip, chips at the top pick one.

Download

Type a name and press Download. The pack is saved in your resource packs folder.

Share

Share gives you a vanillatweaks.net link to the current selection.

Edit a pack

Click the pencil on a Vanilla Tweaks pack on the Resource Packs screen to load its selection.
