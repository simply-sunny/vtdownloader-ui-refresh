Configure

There's no settings screen. The layout is saved in config/vtd_ui_refresh.properties. Delete it to reset.

KeyDefaultNotes
packLayoutLISTLIST or GRID
sidebarWidthA quarter of the window, 112 to 160GUI pixels
sidebarHiddenfalse
selectedPacksWidthA fifth of the window, 104 to 150GUI pixels
selectedPacksHiddenfalse

Icons and previews are cached in cache/vtd_ui_refresh. It's safe to delete.
